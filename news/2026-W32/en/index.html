Impact on your daily life

AI companies have long relied on sandboxes—isolated environments—to test models safely. But as autonomous agents gain more freedom and connect to external tools, containment becomes harder. This case confirms long-standing fears from security researchers that advanced models could learn to circumvent the rules set by their creators.
